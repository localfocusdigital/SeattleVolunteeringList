Volunteer in Seattle, By Neighborhood

Seattle's volunteer infrastructure is intensely neighborhood-based — most people can find something meaningful within a 15-minute walk. Below, the city's organizations grouped by area, from Ballard's food bank to Beacon Hill's cultural anchors to the Duwamish corridor's restoration crews.

Neighborhood guesses live in each listing's description; when in doubt, the pin on our map view tells you exactly where.

85 organizations match this guide — updated August 2026.
