Hospital Volunteering in Seattle

Hospital volunteering is one of the most competitive volunteer categories in the region — healthcare-adjacent roles attract pre-med students and career changers, and programs cap their rosters. Applications typically take 4–8 weeks to process including background checks and health screening.

Most hospital programs ask for roughly one four-hour shift per week over six months to a year. In exchange you get real clinical-adjacent exposure, from patient escorting to unit support.

Making it work

  • Apply to TWO or three hospitals at once — waitlists are real
  • Teens: Overlake, EvergreenHealth and UW run limited summer teen programs with spring deadlines
  • Community health centers (HealthPoint, Sea Mar) are less crowded paths with equally meaningful roles
