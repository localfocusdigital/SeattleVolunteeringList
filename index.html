About this directory

A community-built guide to volunteering across the greater Seattle / Puget Sound region — from Ballard food banks to Chinatown-ID heritage centers to Issaquah trail crews.

Before you go

  • Opportunities change weekly — always confirm on the organization's page.
  • Many roles require an orientation or background check.
  • Youth under 18: look for family/youth-friendly shifts (zoos, museums, parks).
  • Groups of 5+ should email volunteer coordinators directly.
